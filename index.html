Phone app · iOS, Android and web

Focus Garden

A focus timer that grows a tree while you stay off your phone. Leave the app and the tree withers. Finish, and the tree is locked on Bitcoin Cash, then sent to your wallet to keep.

What is in it

  • A voxel valley drawn natively on iOS, Android and the web
  • App blocking through Screen Time and Android usage access
  • A time lock on chain that holds each tree for the full session
  • Small trees that merge into bigger ones, up to a house
  • A leaderboard and public profile cards

Phone app · iOS, Android and web

Venn

A wallet for money people share. Before joining a fund, each member sees what they may spend alone and what power they hand to the others. The rules sit on chain, so nobody can quietly change them later.

What is in it

  • Invitations that show the rules before you join
  • A budget each member can spend alone
  • Group votes for larger payments
  • Lost-phone recovery by the helpers you chose
  • Savings circles with a fixed turn order
