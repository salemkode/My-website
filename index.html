Venn

A Bitcoin Cash wallet for money people share. Before joining a fund, each member sees what they may spend alone and what power they hand to the others. The chain holds everyone to it.

Ready to use Live on mainnet. Solo entry in BCH BLAZE 2026.

Open Venn Read the source

Floors

  1. Savings Circles with a fixed turn order and contribution
  2. Lost-phone recovery by the helpers you chose
  3. Group votes for larger payments, shared as codes
  4. A budget each member can spend alone
  5. Seats accepted with each member's own signature
  6. Invitations that show the rules before you join

Focus Garden

A focus timer that grows a tree while you stay off your phone. Leave the app and the tree withers. Finish, and the tree is locked on Bitcoin Cash, then sent to your wallet to keep.

Ready to use Contracts live on mainnet. BCH BLAZE 2026 entry, rebuilt from the 2025 hackathon project.

Open the app See how it works Read the source

Floors

  1. A leaderboard, public profile cards and tips
  2. Your own wallet: Selene, Paytaca, WalletConnect or WizardConnect
  3. Small trees merge into bigger ones, up to a house
  4. A time lock on chain holds each tree for the full session
  5. Screen Time and usage access block the apps you choose
  6. A voxel valley drawn natively on iOS, Android and web
  7. Plant a tree, stay with it, and it grows

Blaze

A CashTokens indexer to sit behind the explorer: one BCHN node, SQLite and a small REST API. No Chaingraph, Hasura or PostgreSQL to keep alive.

Still being built Installs on Linux. No public endpoint yet.

Read the source

Floors

  1. Backup, and a clean stop and resume every day
  2. Hints for finding a token's BCMR authchain
  3. Token, block, transaction and mempool data over REST
  4. One install command for mainnet and Chipnet
