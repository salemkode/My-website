Main project · online since 2023

Explorer

A block explorer for Bitcoin Cash. Search any block, transaction or address and watch the chain update live. I designed it, built it, and have kept it running and growing for three years.

Open the explorer Source

What is in it

  • Blocks, transactions, addresses and the waiting pool, updating live
  • CashTokens support, with token details read from registries
  • A choice of data servers, with a health check for each one
  • Transaction analytics

Focus Garden

A focus timer that grows a tree while you stay off your phone. Leave the app and the tree withers. Finish, and the tree is yours to keep in your wallet.

  • A voxel valley drawn natively on every platform
  • Blocks the apps you choose while you focus
  • Trees merge into bigger ones, up to a house
